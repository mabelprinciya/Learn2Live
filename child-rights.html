What are Child Rights?

Child rights are the basic rights and protections that help children grow, learn and live safely and with dignity.

Understanding child rights helps children and adults recognize the importance of safety, education, care, equality and protection from harm.

Why are Child Rights important?

Understanding child rights helps promote the safety, education, dignity and well-being of children and encourages respect for their basic protections.
