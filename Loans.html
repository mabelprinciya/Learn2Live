What is a Loan?

A loan is money borrowed from a bank or financial institution that is repaid over an agreed period of time.

Loans can help people manage large expenses such as education, homes, vehicles, or other important needs.

Why are loans important?

Loans can provide financial support when a person needs money for an important planned expense.
