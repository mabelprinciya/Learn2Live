What are Online Scams?

Online scams are deceptive attempts made through the internet to trick people into sharing information, sending money, or taking unsafe actions.

Understanding common scam techniques helps you recognize suspicious messages, websites, calls, emails and online offers before responding to them.

Why is Online Scam Awareness important?

Learning how scams work helps you identify suspicious requests, protect personal information and make safer decisions online.
