What are Basic Legal Rights?

Basic legal rights are rights and protections provided to people under laws and the Constitution. These rights help people live with dignity, equality, freedom and safety.

Understanding basic legal rights helps people know their rights, responsibilities and the proper ways to seek help when their rights are affected.

Why are legal rights important?

Legal rights help protect people from unfair treatment and provide legal protection when problems arise.

Basic Legal Rights

01

Right to Equality

Every person should be treated equally under the law without unfair discrimination.

02

Right to Freedom

People have important freedoms protected by the Constitution, subject to lawful restrictions.

03

Right to Education

Children have the right to free and compulsory education as provided by law.

04

Right to Legal Protection

People can seek legal remedies when their rights are violated or when they face unlawful treatment.

05

Right to Dignity and Respect

Everyone deserves to be treated with dignity and respect and protected from unlawful abuse.
