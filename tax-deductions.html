What are Tax Deductions?

Tax deductions are eligible amounts that may be subtracted from applicable income when determining taxable income under relevant tax rules.

Understanding tax deductions helps you learn how eligible expenses, investments and other permitted deductions may affect the amount of income considered for taxation.

Why are Tax Deductions important?

Understanding tax deductions helps you understand how eligible deductions may reduce taxable income and affect tax calculations.
