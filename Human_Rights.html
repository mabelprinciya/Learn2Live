🌍 Human Rights

📖 Introduction

Human rights are the basic rights and freedoms that belong to every person. They help ensure that everyone is treated with dignity, equality, fairness, and respect.

📚 Important Human Rights

  1. Right to Life – Every person has the right to life and personal security.
  2. Right to Equality – Everyone should be treated equally and fairly.
  3. Right to Freedom – People have basic freedoms, subject to the law.
  4. Right to Education – Everyone should have access to education.
  5. Right to Freedom of Religion – People have the freedom to follow their religion or beliefs.
  6. Right to Dignity – Every person should be treated with respect and dignity.

🛡️ Why Are Human Rights Important?

  • They protect people's dignity and freedom.
  • They promote equality and fairness.
  • They help protect people from discrimination.
  • They encourage respect for every person.
  • They help create a fair and peaceful society.
