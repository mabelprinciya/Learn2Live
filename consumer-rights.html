What are Consumer Rights?

Consumer rights are protections that help people when they buy goods or use services. These rights help consumers make informed choices and receive fair treatment.

Understanding consumer rights helps people check product information, understand prices and terms, keep proof of purchase, and seek help when a product or service does not meet the promised standards.

Why are consumer rights important?

Consumer rights help protect people from unfair practices and support fair and informed transactions.

Basic Consumer Rights

01

Right to Safety

Consumers should be protected from goods and services that may cause harm when used as intended.

02

Right to Information

Consumers should receive important information about products and services before making a purchase.

03

Right to Choose

Consumers should have access to choices between different goods and services at fair prices.

04

Right to Be Heard

Consumers can raise complaints and expect their concerns to be considered through appropriate complaint mechanisms.

05

Right to Seek Redressal

Consumers can seek an appropriate remedy when goods or services do not meet applicable requirements or promises.
