What is Privacy Protection?

Privacy protection means protecting your personal information and controlling how your information is shared and used online.

Understanding privacy protection helps you make safer decisions when using websites, apps, social media and other online services.

Why is Privacy Protection important?

Good privacy practices help reduce unnecessary sharing of personal information and support safer online activity.
