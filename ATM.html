What is an ATM?

ATM stands for Automated Teller Machine. It is an electronic machine that allows bank customers to perform basic banking transactions without visiting a bank counter.

ATMs can be used for services such as cash withdrawal, balance enquiry and other banking services depending on the bank and ATM.

Why is ATM useful?

An ATM allows customers to access basic banking services conveniently without visiting a bank branch.
