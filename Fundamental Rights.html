⚖️ Fundamental Rights

📖 Introduction

Fundamental Rights are the basic rights guaranteed to people by the Constitution of India. They help protect freedom, equality, dignity, and justice. Understanding these rights helps us know our rights and responsibilities as citizens.

📚 Types of Fundamental Rights

  1. Right to Equality – Ensures equality before the law and prohibits discrimination.
  2. Right to Freedom – Provides important freedoms such as freedom of speech and expression, subject to reasonable restrictions.
  3. Right Against Exploitation – Protects people from human trafficking, forced labour, and certain forms of child labour.
  4. Right to Freedom of Religion – Gives people freedom to practise, profess, and propagate religion, subject to constitutional limitations.
  5. Cultural and Educational Rights – Protects the cultural and educational interests of certain groups and minorities.
  6. Right to Constitutional Remedies – Allows people to approach the courts for enforcement of Fundamental Rights.

🛡️ Why Fundamental Rights Are Important

  • They protect individual freedom and dignity.
  • They promote equality and fairness.
  • They protect people from certain forms of exploitation.
  • They support freedom of religion and expression.
  • They provide legal protection when Fundamental Rights are violated.
