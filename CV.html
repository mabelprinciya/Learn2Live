What is an CV?

CV stands for Curriculum Vitae. It is a document that provides information about a person's education, skills, qualifications, achievements, and work experience.

A CV is commonly used when applying for jobs, internships, and other professional opportunities. It helps employers understand a candidate's background and skills.

Why is a CV useful?

A CV helps you present your qualifications, skills and achievements clearly to employers or institutions.
