What is Online Banking?

Online Banking is a digital banking service that allows customers to access and manage their bank accounts using a computer or mobile device.

It can be used to check account information, view transactions, transfer money, and use other banking services without visiting a bank branch.

Why is Online Banking useful?

Online banking makes it convenient to access common banking services from anywhere with a secure internet connection.
