What is a Burn?

A burn is an injury to the skin or other tissues caused by heat, hot liquids, flames, chemicals, electricity, or other sources.

Knowing basic first aid can help protect the affected area and provide appropriate care while waiting for professional medical help when needed.

Why is burn first aid important?

Giving appropriate first aid promptly can help reduce further injury and protect the affected area.

Basic Burn First Aid Steps

01

Move away from the source

Make sure the person is away from the source of the burn and that the area is safe.

02

Cool the burn

Cool the affected area with cool running water as soon as possible.

03

Protect the area

Cover the affected area with a clean, non-stick covering when appropriate.

04

Do not apply harmful substances

Avoid applying ice, creams, oils, or other substances directly to the burn.

05

Get medical help when needed

Seek professional medical help for serious or extensive burns.
