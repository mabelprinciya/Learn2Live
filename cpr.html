What is CPR?

CPR stands for Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation. It is an emergency procedure used when a person is unresponsive and is not breathing normally.

CPR helps maintain blood circulation and oxygen supply to vital organs until emergency medical help takes over.

Why is CPR important?

Starting CPR promptly can help maintain circulation while waiting for professional emergency care.
