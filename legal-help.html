What are Legal Helps?

Cyber rights are support and guidance that help people understand legal issues and know where to seek appropriate assistance. Legal help can include legal information, legal advice, legal aid, and guidance on reporting or resolving legal problems.

Understanding cyber rights helps people protect their personal information, use online services carefully, recognize unsafe activities, and know where to seek help when problems occur.

Why is legal help important?

Cyber rights help people understand their digital protections and support safer and more responsible use of online services.
