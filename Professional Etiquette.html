What is Professional Etiquette?

Professional etiquette refers to the appropriate behaviour, communication, manners, and attitude expected in a professional environment.

It helps people communicate respectfully, work effectively with others, and create a positive impression in the workplace.

Why is Professional Etiquette useful?

Professional etiquette helps you behave respectfully, communicate clearly, and maintain good relationships with colleagues and employers.
