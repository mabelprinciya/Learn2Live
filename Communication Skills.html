What is Communication Skills?

Communication skills are the ability to share information, ideas and thoughts clearly and effectively with others.

Good communication helps you express your ideas confidently, listen carefully and interact effectively with people in academic and professional situations.

Why are Communication Skills useful?

Communication skills help you speak clearly, listen effectively and build positive interactions with others.

Basic Communication Skills

01

Speak clearly

Use simple and clear words so that others can easily understand your message.

02

Listen carefully

Pay attention when others are speaking and try to understand their message.

03

Use positive body language

Maintain good posture, eye contact and appropriate facial expressions.

04

Ask questions

Ask questions when you need clarification and show interest in the conversation.

05

Practice regularly

Practice speaking, listening and expressing your ideas to improve communication skills.
