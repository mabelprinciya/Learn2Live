What is Choking?

Choking happens when a person's airway is blocked by food or another object, making it difficult or impossible to breathe normally.

Knowing basic first aid for choking can help you respond quickly and safely while waiting for professional emergency help.

Why is choking first aid important?

Quick and appropriate action can help a choking person while emergency medical assistance is being arranged.

Basic Choking First Aid Steps

01

Check the situation

Make sure the area is safe and check whether the person is able to breathe, speak, or cough.

02

Encourage coughing

If the person can cough effectively, encourage them to continue coughing to try to clear the blockage.

03

Get emergency help

Get emergency medical help if the person cannot breathe, speak, or cough effectively.

04

Give appropriate first aid

Follow the correct choking first-aid procedure based on the person's age and condition.

05

Continue until help arrives

Continue appropriate first aid and follow emergency instructions until trained medical help takes over.
