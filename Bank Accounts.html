What is a Bank Account?

A bank account is an account maintained by a bank to keep and manage a person's money safely.

Bank accounts help people save money, receive money, make payments, withdraw money, and manage their everyday financial activities.

Why is a bank account important?

A bank account provides a safe place to keep money and makes it easier to save, receive, and use money.

Types of Bank Accounts

01

Savings Account

A savings account is mainly used to save money and earn interest.

02

Current Account

A current account is generally used for frequent transactions and business activities.

03

Fixed Deposit Account

A fixed deposit allows money to be deposited for a fixed period and generally earns interest.

04

Recurring Deposit Account

A recurring deposit allows a person to deposit a fixed amount regularly for a specific period.
